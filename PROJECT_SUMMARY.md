## 🎯 Features Implemented

### 1. Manajemen Mahasiswa
- ✅ Create student (POST /api/students)
- ✅ Get all students (GET /api/students)
- ✅ Get student by ID (GET /api/students/:id)
- ✅ Delete student (DELETE /api/students/:id)
- ✅ Validasi NIM (10 digit, unique)
- ✅ Validasi nama (min 3 karakter)

### 2. Sistem Penilaian
- ✅ Add grade (POST /api/students/:id/grades)
- ✅ Validasi score (0-100)
- ✅ Auto-calculate letter grade (A, B, C, D, E)
- ✅ Auto-calculate GPA (0.0 - 4.0)
- ✅ Determine student status (Cumlaude, Sangat Memuaskan, etc.)

### 3. Analytics
- ✅ Get top students (GET /api/students/top)
- ✅ Ranking by GPA
- ✅ Filter by major
